# Game UI accessibility

Keep menus and HUD text readable at supported phone sizes and text scales.
Use at least 48 logical pixels for touch targets, safe areas around system
insets, and semantics for interactive Flutter controls. Pixel art does not
justify illegible text or controls. Communicate important states beyond color
or audio alone. Respect reduced-motion preferences where applicable and provide
music/SFX controls when audio exists. Verify real interactions and overflow;
do not impose FlashKids audience, mascot or child-specific restrictions.
